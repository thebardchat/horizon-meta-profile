# Friend / family pitch — the 3-line ask

For close people who'd say yes to "help me out" but who don't necessarily live in VR or follow your work-work. Goal: a follow + maybe a share. Low-effort, no headset required.

## The ask (text / DM)

```
hey — I started a creator account on Meta Horizon (the VR thing). takes ~30 seconds to follow from your phone or browser, no headset needed:

https://horizon.meta.com/profile/702118972988489/

if it's not your thing, no worries — share it with anyone you know who's into VR/AI/storytelling and that helps too 🙏
```

## The ask (voice / in-person)

> "Hey, when you have a second — I started a creator profile on Meta Horizon. You can follow me from your phone, takes thirty seconds, and it really helps the algorithm pick me up. Link's in the message I'll send."

Then send the text version above.

## Who to ask, in priority order

1. **People who actively use Meta accounts already** (Instagram, Facebook, Quest owners) — their follow has more weight inside the Meta graph than a cold follow from a brand-new account
2. **People in creative/tech communities** — they'll signal-boost to like-minded folks
3. **Family** — they'll do it because they love you, even if they never use Horizon

## What NOT to do

- **Don't mass-blast.** Send individually, ideally with one personalized line. Mass messages read as spam and convert poorly.
- **Don't ask twice.** If they don't respond in a week, drop it. Pestering torches goodwill.
- **Don't promise anything in return.** Keep it as a simple ask, not a transactional favor.

## Tracking

If you want to know whether a specific friend's share converted, generate a custom share link from inside the Horizon mobile app rather than reusing this one — see `tracking.md`.
