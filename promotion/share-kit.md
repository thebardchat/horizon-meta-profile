## Don'ts

- Don't post the same exact copy on multiple platforms within the same hour — algorithms downrank cross-posted-as-is content. Vary at least 1 sentence.
- Don't strip the `utm_*` params unless you have a reason (analytics breaks).
- Don't promote the profile in unrelated communities — that's spam, and the constitution doesn't allow it.
